💡 What is Sustainability?

Sustainability means using resources wisely, protecting the environment, and living in a way that keeps the planet healthy for the long run.

Importance Of Sustainability

  • Decrease Of Water & Air Pollution
  • Protects forests & oceans
  • Helps preserve natural resources for the future
  • Improves quality of life & Health
  • Supports long‑term environmental balance

🌱 Major Factors Of Sustainability

♻ Reduce Waste

Reusing Bags & Recycling Plastic helps to protect the environment.

🌞 Save Energy

Using LED bulbs and using renewable energy reduces pollution.

💧 Protect Water

Saving water ensures clean water is available for everyone & Decrease the chance of spreading dieases.
