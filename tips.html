Eco-Friendly Tips to follow

Here are easy everyday habits that help protect the planet.

💡
Turn off lights

Save energy by switching off lights and appliances when not in use.

🛍
Use cloth bags

Reduce plastic waste by carrying reusable shopping bags.

♻
Proper Recycle

Sort paper, plastic, glass and metal to help reduce landfill waste.

🚰
Save water

Turn off taps while not in use.

🚶‍♀
Walk or cycle

Reduce carbon emissions by choosing eco-friendly transport.

🌱
Plant trees

Trees improve air quality and produce more oxygen.

🔄
Reuse items

Reusebottles and bags.

🔋
Use LED bulbs

LED lights use less energy.

🛒
Correct Eco products

Look for reusable or energy-saving items.

📣
Spread habits

Share habits with your family.
