✨ 10 Simple Eco-Friendly Tips

10 Small steps you can do to help protect the Planet Earth

💡
Turn off lights

Save energy by switching off lights and appliances when not in use.

🛍
Use cloth bags

Reduce plastic waste by carrying reusable shopping bags (cloth bags).

♻
Recycle properly

Sort paper, plastic, glass and metal to help reduce landfill waste.

🚰
Save water

Turn off taps while not in use.

🚶
Walk, Cycle Or EV

Reduce carbon emissions by choosing eco-friendly transport.

🌱
Plant trees

Trees improve air quality and support wildlife ecosystems.

🔄
Reuse items

Reuse Bottles and bags instead of throwing them away.

🔋
Use LED bulbs

LED lights use less energy and last longer.

🛒
Choose eco products

Look for reusable or energy-saving items.

📣
Spread awareness

Share eco-friendly habits with friends and family.
